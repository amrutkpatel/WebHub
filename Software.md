**01 — NucleoDesk**

**Molecular Biology & DNA Sequence Analysis**

NucleoDesk is a Windows desktop application for DNA sequence analysis and molecular biology workflows, including plasmid visualization, cloning design, primer planning, restriction digest analysis and chromatogram review. NucleoDesk was formerly known as NucleoMap.

**02 — CHPVWeb**

**Chandipura Virus: What We Know and What We Don't Know**

CHPVWeb is a web-based scientific resource focused on Chandipura virus (CHPV), presenting information about what is currently known about the virus and highlighting areas where knowledge remains limited or uncertain.

**04 — KidPilot Learning**

**Offline Learning App for Children**

KidPilot Learning is a free standalone offline Android learning application for children, with lessons in English, Hindi, Gujarati, mathematics and animal science for Grades 1–4.

The app includes speech-supported learning and speaking practice and is designed to work without login, advertisements, subscriptions or server synchronization in the public build.
